How it works

Three steps, one thumb.

Cleaning a gallery is a thousand small decisions. GalleryShift makes each one a swipe.

Swipe

One photo or video at a time. Right keeps it, left sets it aside. A quick flick counts, and Undo brings the last card back.

Review the bin

Everything you swiped left waits in the bin. Tap anything to rescue it, then delete the rest in one go.

Get the space back

See how much you freed. On Android 11 and newer, deleted photos stay in your phone’s trash for 30 days in case you change your mind.

Questions

Good to know.

Is anything deleted when I swipe left?

No. Swiping left only moves the photo to the review bin inside the app. Nothing is removed from your phone until you open the bin and tap Delete, and Android asks you to confirm.

Can I get a deleted photo back?

On Android 11 and newer, yes, for 30 days: deleted photos go to your phone’s trash, which you can open from your gallery or Files app. Android 10 and older have no trash, so the app tells you the delete is permanent and asks first. Files removed in Storage cleanup are always deleted permanently.

Why does Storage cleanup ask for “All files access”?

Leftover trash, old installers, half-finished downloads and empty folders are not part of your photo library, and Android only lets an app find them with this access. It is optional: swiping photos works without it. The app never looks inside other apps’ private folders, and you can turn the access off again in Android Settings.

Does it upload my photos anywhere?

No. Everything happens on your phone. The app does not even have permission to use the internet.

Is it free?

Yes. There are no ads and no in-app purchases.

GalleryShift is available for Android only at the moment.

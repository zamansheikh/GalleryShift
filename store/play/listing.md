# Play Store listing

Text to paste into Play Console, Main store listing. Written for version 1.0.0.

## App name (30 characters at most)

```
GalleryShift: Photo Cleaner
```

## Short description (80 characters at most)

```
Swipe right to keep, left to delete. Clean up photos and videos and free space.
```

## Full description (4000 characters at most)

```
Thousands of photos and no idea where to start? GalleryShift turns cleaning your gallery into a simple swipe.

Go through your photos and videos one at a time. Swipe right to keep, swipe left to let go. Nothing is deleted until you review the bin and say so.

HOW IT WORKS
• Swipe right to keep a photo, left to set it aside
• Changed your mind? Undo brings the last card back
• Open the review bin, rescue anything you want, then delete the rest in one go
• See how much space you freed

DECKS THAT MAKE SENSE
Pick up where you left off, a few minutes at a time.
• All photos, newest first
• Shuffle, for a random mix
• On this day
• Screenshots
• Videos
• One deck for every month, each with its own progress
Finished a month and want another look? Review it again without starting your whole library over.

VIDEOS PLAY RIGHT ON THE CARD
No need to open each video to decide. Videos start on their own, muted.
• Drag the seek bar to scrub
• Double-tap either side to jump 10 seconds
• Hold to play at 4× speed
• Go full screen and carry on from the same spot

A SAFETY NET
• Swiping left only moves a photo to the review bin. It stays on your phone until you delete it
• You always confirm before anything is removed
• On Android 11 and newer, deleted photos go to your phone's trash for 30 days, so you can still get them back
• On Android 10 and older, which have no trash, the app tells you the delete is permanent and asks first

STORAGE CLEANUP
An optional tool for the rest of your storage. Turn on All files access and GalleryShift finds:
• Junk: leftovers from the trash, old app installers (APK), temp files, half-finished downloads and thumbnail caches
• Empty folders
• Large files over 50 MB, biggest first
• Documents such as PDFs, Office files and ebooks. Tap one to open it and check before deciding
You choose what goes, and the app asks before deleting. Files removed here are deleted permanently. It never looks inside other apps' private folders.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Your photos never leave your phone
• No account, no sign-in
• No ads, no analytics, no tracking
• The app has no permission to use the internet

MADE TO FEEL GOOD
• Light and dark themes that follow your phone
• Smooth cards with haptic feedback
• Your progress is saved as you go

GalleryShift is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

Questions or ideas? Visit https://zamansheikh.github.io/GalleryShift/support/
```
